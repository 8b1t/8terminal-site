What it does

Terminal

  • Tabs and split panes on the Mac
  • Reconnects and reattaches tmux or zellij
  • A notification when a coding agent waits for you
  • Paste a screenshot or file; its path lands at the prompt
  • Themes, a theme per host, Nerd Font symbols

Keys and sign-in

  • Passwords, Ed25519, ECDSA and RSA keys, two-factor prompts
  • SSH agent and Secure Enclave keys on the Mac
  • OpenSSH certificates from your CA
  • Agent forwarding that asks before every signature
  • Jump host chains and per-host environment

Files and tunnels

  • SFTP over the session you already opened
  • Folders, permissions, drag in from Finder
  • Transfers that resume after a dropped connection
  • Local, remote and SOCKS5 forwarding

Your vault

  • Unlock with Touch ID, Face ID or your master password
  • Several vaults; encrypted backup to your own iCloud Drive or Google Drive — free
  • Import from Termius, MobaXterm, PuTTY, Tabby and OpenSSH config
  • Readable JSON export without secrets

iPhone and iPad

  • A key row for Esc, Ctrl, Alt, arrows and F-keys
  • Live Activity with Return, Ctrl-C and Reconnect
  • Home Screen widget and Shortcuts
  • Hardware keyboard shortcuts on iPad

AI assistant, your key

  • Bring your own provider key; it stays on your device
  • By default you choose what it reads, and a suggested command runs only after you approve it, once
  • Optional Auto mode, off until you turn it on: it runs read-only checks on your Linux server by itself and asks before anything that changes the server
  • In Auto mode what those checks print is sent to your AI provider, and you agree before the first run
